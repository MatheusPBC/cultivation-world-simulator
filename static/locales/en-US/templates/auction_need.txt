Requirements:
1. Decide only for this one cultivator. Do not mention or decide for anybody else.
2. Address each lot by its exact lot_key string. Two lots may share the same item_id; they are still different lots and are judged separately.
3. The need level must be one of the integers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 — nothing else. 1 means completely unneeded, 5 means absolutely needed.
4. Return a plain integer, never a decimal, never a quoted string, and never a value outside 1-5. Any other value is rejected and counts as no bid.
5. You may omit a lot; an omitted lot simply counts as no bid.
6. Judge from this cultivator's own circumstances. For example, someone near the end of their lifespan needs a longevity elixir most.
